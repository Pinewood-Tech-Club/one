---
title: Reporting Bugs
description: To report a bug, just email us!
---

We're only human, and we make mistakes. We'll try to catch most bugs before they reach you, but sometimes they slip through :(

If you encounter any bugs or glitches while using Pinewood One, please email us at techclub@pinewood.edu so we can fix it as soon as possible!

We would appreciate it if you could include as much detail as possible in your email, such as:

- What you were doing when the bug occurred
- What you expected to happen
- What actually happened
- Any error messages you received

If you are able to reproduce the bug and can get a screen recording, that would be even better!

We read every email, and will get back to you as soon as possible.

-tech club :)
